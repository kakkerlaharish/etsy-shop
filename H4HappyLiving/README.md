# H4HappyLiving

H4HappyLiving is a family-inspired lifestyle brand creating practical, beautiful digital and physical products that help people **Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive**.

## Roadmap

1. **Etsy digital downloads:** validate evergreen products and build customer trust.
2. **Print-on-demand:** expand proven designs into journals, wall art, home goods, and select apparel.
3. **Multi-platform:** grow through Amazon, Shopify, email, and a direct website.

## Project map

- `docs/` — operating playbooks and standards
- `products/` — one self-contained folder per product; pillars are recorded as metadata rather than used as folder levels
- `assets/` — reusable brand files, mockups, and photography
- `templates/` — repeatable product and listing documents
- `research/` — EverBee, competitor, keyword, and trend research
- `automation/` — future workflows and scripts

## Product principles

Every product should solve a real problem, be easy to use, feel clean and modern, offer immediate value, remain evergreen where possible, and have potential to grow into bundles and other marketplaces.

## Product organization

Each product is managed as its own project under `products/`. Its folder contains the requirements, research, source designs, customer deliverables, Etsy listing assets, reusable product-specific templates, marketing material, and archive.

Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive remain the brand pillars used to classify products. They should be recorded in the product README and tracking documents, not used as an extra directory layer. Shared brand assets and reusable business-wide templates remain in `assets/` and `templates/`.

## Canva workspace

The Canva account follows the same product-first model. **H4HappyLiving → Product** contains one folder per actual product and intentionally has no pillar-based parent folders. Shared brand assets live under Brand, reusable master layouts under Templates, channel-level work under Designs, and multi-product promotions under Sales.

The retired empty pillar folders are preserved under **Archive → Legacy Product Categories** for recovery only. See `docs/06_ai_workflows/canva_workflow.md` for the live Canva structure, folder links, and placement rules.
