# H4HappyLiving

H4HappyLiving is a family-inspired lifestyle brand creating practical, beautiful digital and physical products that help people **Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive**.

## Roadmap

1. **Etsy digital downloads:** validate evergreen products and build customer trust.
2. **Print-on-demand:** expand proven designs into journals, wall art, home goods, and select apparel.
3. **Multi-platform:** grow through Amazon, Shopify, email, and a direct website.

## Project map

- `docs/` — operating playbooks and standards
- `products/` — product work organized by the four brand pillars
- `assets/` — reusable brand files, mockups, and photography
- `templates/` — repeatable product and listing documents
- `research/` — EverBee, competitor, keyword, and trend research
- `automation/` — future workflows and scripts

## Product principles

Every product should solve a real problem, be easy to use, feel clean and modern, offer immediate value, remain evergreen where possible, and have potential to grow into bundles and other marketplaces.

## Brand promise

Practical, thoughtful products for everyday life.
