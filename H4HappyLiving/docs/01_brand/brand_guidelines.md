# Brand Guidelines

## Brand foundation

**H4HappyLiving** helps individuals and families organize, learn, create, and thrive through thoughtful digital and physical products.

## Design direction

Use a clean, modern, warm, and family-oriented visual style. Prioritize generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, plain language, and accessible contrast. Final colors, fonts, and logo files belong in `assets/brand/` once chosen.

## Product pillars

- **Organize:** simplify everyday life.
- **Learn:** make learning practical and enjoyable.
- **Create:** encourage expression and personalization.
- **Thrive:** support healthier habits and meaningful progress.

## Guardrails

Do not use copyrighted characters, logos, artwork, fonts, or phrases without a valid license. Keep each product useful before decorative.
