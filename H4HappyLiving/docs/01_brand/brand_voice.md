# Brand Voice

## Voice traits

Friendly, trustworthy, modern, practical, encouraging, family-oriented, and clear.

## Writing rules

- Lead with the customer benefit.
- Use direct, plain language.
- Explain what is included and how it helps.
- Avoid exaggerated promises, pressure, and jargon.
- Be supportive and specific in customer messages.

## Example

“Bring a little more calm to your week with an easy-to-use family meal planner.”
