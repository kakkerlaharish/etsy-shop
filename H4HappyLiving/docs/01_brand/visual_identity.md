# H4HappyLiving Visual Identity

## Creative idea

**A happier rhythm for everyday life.**

The identity combines a simple home shape with four rounded windows. The home represents everyday living; the four windows represent Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive. Rounded geometry feels friendly, while deep evergreen gives the brand trust and maturity.

## Logo system

- **Primary logo:** symbol plus wordmark for headers, websites, packaging, and title pages.
- **Brand mark:** home-and-four-windows symbol for avatars, favicons, stickers, and small placements.
- **One-color logo:** Evergreen, Ink, or white when color reproduction is limited.
- Keep clear space equal to one window around the logo.
- Minimum digital sizes: 180 px wide for the primary logo and 28 px square for the mark.
- Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, or place the logo over a busy image.

Editable SVG files live in `assets/brand/logos/`.

### Color balance

- Use Warm Cream or white for approximately 60–70% of a typical layout.
- Use Evergreen, Ink, or Soft Sage for approximately 20–30%.
- Keep the active pillar accent near 10%.
- Use only one dominant pillar accent in a single product or campaign design.
- Evergreen remains the master-brand color even when a pillar accent is prominent.

### Accessibility

- Use Ink or Evergreen text on Warm Cream or white.
- Use white text on Evergreen or Ink.
- Pair accent-color backgrounds with Ink text.
- Do not rely on color alone to identify a pillar; include its name or icon.

## Typography

**Approved system:** Nunito Sans with Lora as a limited editorial accent. The following styles were installed in the H4HappyLiving Canva Brand Kit on 2026-07-30.

| Canva role | Family | Style | Default size | Use |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Title | Nunito Sans | Ultra Bold | 42 | Covers, hero messages, major campaign titles |
| Subtitle | Nunito Sans | Semi Bold | 32 | Supporting hero message |
| Heading | Nunito Sans | Bold | 28 | Main page or listing section heading |
| Subheading | Nunito Sans | Semi Bold | 24 | Secondary section heading |
| Section header | Nunito Sans | Bold | 20 | Labels and compact section titles |
| Body | Nunito Sans | Regular | 16 | Instructions, descriptions, and paragraphs |
| Quote | Lora | Regular Italic | 18 | Short reflective or editorial accent only |
| Caption | Nunito Sans | Regular | 12 | Captions, metadata, and secondary notes |

- Use no more than two font families in one design.
- Use Nunito Sans for all functional, instructional, marketplace, and small text.
- Reserve Lora Italic for one or two short lines; never use it for instructions, specifications, buttons, or captions.
- Prefer sentence case. Reserve all caps for very short labels with generous spacing.
- Maintain visible size contrast between title, heading, body, and caption roles.
- Fallbacks: `"Nunito Sans", "Avenir Next", Arial, sans-serif` and `Lora, Georgia, serif`.
- Both families are available under open licenses. Keep license files with distributed font files.

## Layout and imagery

- Use generous margins, calm layouts, and rounded rectangles.
- Use simple line icons with rounded ends and consistent strokes.
- Keep patterns quiet: four-dot grids, small windows, leaves, arcs, and soft sunbursts.
- Show real, calm everyday moments in warm daylight with natural materials and diverse households.
- Avoid sterile stock imagery, heavy filters, clutter, and staged perfection.
- Product usefulness and reading order always come before decoration.

## Product-pillar coding

| Pillar | Color | Visual cue | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organize | Golden Joy | grid/check | clarity and momentum |
| Learn | Sky | open page/spark | curiosity and growth |
