# Business Context

## Mission

Create thoughtful, practical, and beautiful products that help people organize their lives, continue learning, express creativity, and thrive every day.

## Audience

Primary: families, parents, professionals, and students.

Secondary: teachers, small-business owners, entrepreneurs, homemakers, and gift shoppers.

## Business model

Begin with Etsy digital downloads. Use demand, competition, reviews, profit margin, update effort, bundle potential, and print-on-demand potential to decide what to build next.

## Long-term vision

Build a recognizable, trusted lifestyle brand across Etsy, print-on-demand, Amazon, Shopify, and owned channels while maintaining high quality and customer satisfaction.
