# Etsy Shop Setup Requirements

> Verify requirements and fees in Etsy’s current help center before submitting information; they vary by seller location and can change.

## Prerequisites

- Etsy account and desktop browser for initial shop setup.
- Shop language, country, and pricing currency selected deliberately. Etsy says the default shop language cannot later be changed.
- A shop name that is 4–20 characters, has no spaces or special characters, is unique, and does not infringe another trademark.
- Eligible Etsy Payments location, bank account, and billing debit or credit card.
- Government photo ID and a clear selfie for identity verification.
- Tax and business information appropriate to the seller’s location and business type.
- Two-factor authentication, preferably with an authenticator app.

## Setup sequence

1. Visit [Etsy Sell](https://www.etsy.com/sell) on a desktop browser and create or sign in to an Etsy account.
2. Choose preferences and reserve a compliant shop name.
3. Complete Etsy Payments, bank verification, billing card, legal/tax, and identity verification.
4. Pay any onboarding fee presented by Etsy; amounts and availability vary.
5. Configure storefront details, About section, shop policies, and digital-buyer message.
6. Create and review the first listing, then open the shop.
7. Store backup codes securely outside the repository.

## Digital-download rules

Digital products must be made and/or designed by the seller. Etsy currently supports up to five files per instant-download listing, each up to 20 MB. File names are buyer-visible and are limited to 70 permitted characters. Digital listings do not support variations.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Test every file and link after export.
- [ ] Confirm file names, instructions, and license language are clear.
- [ ] Add accurate title, category, attributes, description, tags, images, and price.
- [ ] Confirm policies and customer-contact information.
- [ ] Review the shop on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Set a regular financial, customer-service, and performance review cadence.
