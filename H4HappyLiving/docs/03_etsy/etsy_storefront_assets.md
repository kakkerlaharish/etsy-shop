## Image rules that apply across the shop

- Etsy supports `.jpg`, `.gif`, `.png`, `.svg`, and `.heic` for shop images generally.
- Animated GIFs and transparent PNGs are not supported. Transparent areas can appear black.
- Export web images in the sRGB color profile. Etsy converts uploaded images to sRGB and may compress them.
- Preview every asset on desktop and mobile after upload.

## 1. Shop logo

### Etsy requirements and guidance

- Use a square image at least **500 x 500 px**; Etsy recommends **500 x 500 px**.
- For the logo field in Shop basics, Etsy specifies `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.gif` and a file smaller than **10 MB**.
- Etsy provides a square crop tool during upload.
- Keep the design simple, recognizable at large and small sizes, and free of long text or busy backgrounds.
- Etsy notes that a missing shop logo may affect search visibility.

## 3. Shop story and About section

### Etsy requirements and guidance

- The **About your shop** story can contain up to **5,000 characters**.
- Etsy recommends explaining the business background, creative process, story behind the products, collaborators, and future direction.
- The About section may include one featured video and up to five featured photos with captions.
- Featured video formats: MP4, MOV, AVI, MPEG, or M4V, up to **300 MB**.
- Featured photos: `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.gif`, up to **2 MB** each. Etsy crops them to display at **760 x 468 px**.
- Links may point buyers to the shop's website, blog, or social profiles.
- Etsy recommends using the section to demonstrate experience, process, and trustworthiness rather than repeating listing copy.
- Etsy says an unopened shop must be opened before its public About section can be edited.

### H4HappyLiving content plan

Use this order:

1. The everyday problem H4HappyLiving helps solve.
2. The four pillars: Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive.
3. How products are researched, designed, reviewed, and tested.
4. The standard customers can expect: useful, clear, flexible, and visually calm.
5. A short, factual note about the owner and any collaborators.
6. The future direction from digital downloads to carefully selected physical products.

### Approved starter copy

H4HappyLiving creates thoughtful, practical products designed to make everyday life feel more manageable, meaningful, and joyful.

Our work is organized around four parts of daily life: Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive. From clear planning tools and inviting learning resources to creative projects and gentle wellbeing supports, each product begins with a real need and is designed to be simple to start, pleasant to use, and flexible enough for real routines.

We value clear instructions, accessible layouts, calm modern design, and honest product details. Every item is reviewed for usefulness and ease of use before it reaches the shop.

H4HappyLiving is beginning with digital downloads and plans to grow proven ideas into carefully selected physical products over time. Our goal is simple: practical joy for everyday life.

Before publishing, add a truthful first-person owner introduction and any specific process details that can be supported. Do not invent a founding date, credentials, team members, production methods, or personal story.

### Suggested About media

- Workspace or design-process overview.
- A product being tested or used in a real setting.
- A representative product range across the four pillars.
- Detail shot showing typography, layout, or print quality.
- Owner working on or reviewing a product.

Use landscape source images composed to survive Etsy's 760 x 468 crop.

### Editing path

`Shop Manager -> Settings -> Your shop -> Shop home editor -> About`

## 4. Seller photo

### Etsy requirements and guidance

- Use a square profile image at least **400 x 400 px** and smaller than **10 MB**.
- Etsy accepts `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.gif` for profile pictures.
- A non-square original may appear distorted.
- A profile picture cannot be deleted after it is added, but it can be replaced.
- Etsy requires all people involved in the shop, including the owner, to be listed in the About section with their role. A shop-team entry includes a photo, name, role, and bio.

### H4HappyLiving production specification

- Use a current, clear head-and-shoulders photo of the actual shop owner—not the H4HappyLiving logo.
- Photograph in soft natural light against a simple, warm, uncluttered background.
- Keep the face centered with enough space around it for Etsy's circular or square crops.
- Avoid filters, embedded text, watermarks, group photos, and distracting backgrounds.
- Export a **1000 x 1000 px** sRGB JPG or PNG under 10 MB.
- Suggested filename: `h4happyliving-owner-photo-1000x1000.jpg`.
- Add a short owner bio that truthfully explains the owner's role in designing and running the shop.

### Upload path

From the storefront checklist, choose **Upload a seller photo**. The public account profile can also be edited through `Your account -> Account settings -> Public profile`.

Shop-team details are managed through `Shop Manager -> Settings -> Your shop -> Shop team`.
