# Product Development Workflow

## Workflow

1. Create a product folder from `templates/product_folder_template.md` and record its primary brand pillar in the product README.
2. Validate demand with EverBee and Etsy research.
3. Review leading competitors, buyer needs, and gaps.
4. Choose a focused keyword opportunity.
5. Write product requirements and define the customer problem.
6. Design, test, and quality-check the product.
7. Create compliant files and professional mockups.
8. Prepare the listing, including title, tags, description, category, attributes, and price.
9. Launch, monitor performance, and collect feedback.
10. Improve, bundle, and consider POD or cross-platform expansion.

## Organization rule

Treat each product as a self-contained project. Store its research, source designs, deliverables, Etsy listing material, templates, mockups, and marketing inside that product's folder. Use Organize, Learn, Create, and Thrive as classification metadata rather than parent folders.

Keep only genuinely shared material at the project level: brand assets in `assets/brand/`, general mockup or photography resources in `assets/`, and reusable business-wide templates in `templates/`.

Mirror the same workspace in Canva under **H4HappyLiving → Product**. Follow `docs/06_ai_workflows/canva_workflow.md` for the implemented folder structure, placement rules, links, and legacy archive.

## Product decision scorecard

Assess search demand, competition opportunity, evergreen value, problem solved, bundle potential, POD potential, profit margin, ease of updates, and cross-platform potential.

## Definition of ready

A product is ready to publish only when the deliverables, instructions, file names, listing copy, images, price, and customer-use test are complete.
