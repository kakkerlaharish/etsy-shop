## Creative contribution

- Opportunity or idea: Give each role its own form of creativity rather than applying a vague project-wide creativity requirement.
- Why it is distinctive: Each agent contributes a different kind of originality—system design, exploration, synthesis, experience design, visual expression, or merchandising.
- Evidence or customer need: Clear creative ownership reduces overlap and helps ideas survive handoffs without being mistaken for evidence or approved scope.
- Practical value: Agents can work independently while producing more original and commercially useful work that the Business Owner can evaluate explicitly.
- Tradeoffs or risks: Creative proposals could create scope creep if their approval status is unclear; the updated handoff template prevents this by requiring status and tradeoffs.
