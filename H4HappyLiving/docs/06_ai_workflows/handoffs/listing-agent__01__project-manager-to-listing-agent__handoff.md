- Opportunity or idea: Unify search intent, conversion copy, and image merchandising under one specialist so the buyer receives one coherent listing story.
- Why it is distinctive: The role connects the phrase that attracts the buyer with the benefit, product proof, image sequence, expectations, and FAQs that help the buyer decide.
- Evidence or customer need: Etsy discovery and conversion depend on both relevance and immediate buyer clarity; splitting those buyer-facing decisions across unrelated owners can create conflicting messages.
- Practical value: Produces one reviewable offline listing package while preserving Business Owner approval and Shop Manager control over external actions.
- Tradeoffs or risks: The role could overlap research, brand, or product work unless it uses approved inputs and routes new evidence, asset, scope, price, or design needs to their owners.
