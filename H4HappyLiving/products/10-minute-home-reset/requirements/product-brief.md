# Product brief: The 10-Minute Home Reset

## Customer and use case

The product supports busy adults and households that want a calmer-looking space but may feel overwhelmed by long, open-ended cleaning routines. It should help a customer begin quickly, make visible progress, and stop without guilt when the timer ends.

## Scope

The bundle contains six pages:

1. Cover and quick-start instructions
2. Ten-minute whole-home reset
3. Five-minute low-energy reset
4. Room-by-room reset matrix
5. Weekly reset tracker
6. Blank personal reset planner

## Formats

- PDF, static and non-editable
- US Letter: 8.5 x 11 inches
- A4: 210 x 297 millimeters
- Full-color and printer-friendly editions

## Design requirements

- Follow the approved Calm Everyday palette.
- Use Evergreen and Ink for text, Warm Cream or white for most backgrounds, Soft Sage for structure, and Golden Joy as the Organize accent.
- Use the approved Arial fallback because Nunito Sans font files are not installed locally.
- Maintain generous margins, clear reading order, rounded shapes, and accessible contrast.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
- Use only original vector shapes and text; no third-party artwork is required.

## Copy requirements

- Use warm, practical, encouraging US English.
- Avoid guilt, perfection language, urgency, and medical claims.
- Describe the product as an organizational aid, not treatment.
- Use ASCII hyphens throughout the PDFs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Four correctly sized PDF files are present.
- Each PDF contains exactly six pages.
- Pages render without clipped or overlapping text, broken glyphs, or unintended blank pages.
- Headers, footers, page numbers, checkboxes, and writing lines remain aligned.
- The printer-friendly edition is usable without color.
- Customer-facing filenames are clear and under Etsy's filename limit.
