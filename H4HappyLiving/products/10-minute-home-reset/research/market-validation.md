# Market validation notes

Reviewed: July 30, 2026

## Decision

Proceed with a focused home-reset printable instead of a generic cleaning planner. The narrower promise is easier to understand, quicker to develop, and more differentiated while remaining inside an established cleaning-checklist market.

## Public demand signals

- Etsy buyers have left positive reviews for ADHD-friendly cleaning checklists, including comments about staying on track and reducing overwhelm.
- Comparable cleaning and reset printables commonly appear between roughly $2 and $6.
- A visible 10-minute room reset listing confirms the time-boxed positioning is already recognizable to buyers.

## Positioning choice

The product copy leads with a gentle 10-minute reset rather than a diagnosis. ADHD-related search language may be tested in Etsy tags, but the product makes no medical or therapeutic claims.

## Validation limitation

Public Etsy pages do not expose reliable exact keyword search volume. Before publishing, confirm the final search phrases in Etsy Marketplace Insights and compare impressions after launch.
