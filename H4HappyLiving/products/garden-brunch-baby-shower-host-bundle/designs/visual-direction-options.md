## Option A — Garden Table (recommended)

- Commercial signal: The clearest expression of “one calm kit from invitation to shower day.” It is refined enough for the $24 regular test price without narrowing the buyer to a strongly gendered theme.
- Visual system: Warm Cream ground, Evergreen/Ink type, Soft Sage fields, Fresh Leaf accents, an airy arched garden frame, and sparse line-drawn sprigs.
- Layout behavior: Centered information column with generous margins; high legibility for print, mobile adaptation, signs, and games.
- H4HappyLiving distinction: Quiet garden architecture and four-stage host navigation rather than an illustration-heavy floral invitation.
- Watch-outs: Keep botanical elements simple and licensed for Canva Free; prevent the arch from becoming a generic boho motif.

## Option B — Citrus & Sage Brunch

- Commercial signal: The fastest theme recognition in an Etsy thumbnail; sunny citrus cues communicate brunch immediately.
- Visual system: Warm Cream, Evergreen, Soft Sage, restrained Golden Joy citrus, and one tiny Coral highlight; simplified citrus leaves and blossoms framing a clear central column.
- Layout behavior: Asymmetrical corners create energy while the center remains easy to edit and scale.
- H4HappyLiving distinction: Functional, modern citrus geometry rather than watercolor lemons, gingham, or rustic-market styling.
- Watch-outs: Citrus may read as a general party or bridal shower unless the baby-shower purpose and host-system promise dominate the hierarchy.
- Canva status: Build-ready specification; preview generation blocked by the current Canva quota.

## Option C — Pressed Meadow Editorial

- Commercial signal: The most premium and editorial direction; strongest fit for buyers who want refined stationery rather than playful shower graphics.
- Visual system: Warm Cream, Evergreen, Ink, Soft Sage, restrained Create Coral, sparse flat pressed-flower silhouettes, fine dividers, and a quiet four-dot/window-grid motif.
- Layout behavior: Disciplined editorial grid with the best content hierarchy for long names and venues.
- H4HappyLiving distinction: Connects the brand's four-window rhythm to botanical stationery without copying competitor floral compositions.
- Watch-outs: May appear less obviously “brunch” in a small thumbnail; listing imagery would need a clear table-setting mockup and brunch label.
- Canva status: Build-ready specification; preview generation blocked by the current Canva quota.

## Selection recommendation

Choose **Option A — Garden Table** for the MVP. It has the broadest gender-inclusive appeal, the strongest extension path across every required asset, and the most direct connection between the calm visual system and the host-first promise. Choose Option B if immediate brunch-theme recognition is more important than long-term product-family flexibility. Choose Option C if premium editorial positioning is the priority and the listing can carry more of the brunch storytelling.

No candidate has been converted into a saved Canva design. After selection, create only the approved candidate or foundation, place it in the product's Canva `Designs` folder, and build the first proof set: 5 x 7 invitation, mobile invitation, one insert, one sign, and one game. Review that proof set before scaling to the full bundle.
