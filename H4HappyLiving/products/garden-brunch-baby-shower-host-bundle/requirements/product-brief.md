## Customer and problem

- Target customer: A friend, sibling, parent, or co-host planning a polished, modern baby shower without professional design or event-production help
- Customer problem: Separate invitations, inserts, games, signs, and print instructions create too many design and file-management decisions.
- Product promise: One calm Canva kit that takes the host from invitation to shower day.
- Intended use: Personal baby-shower planning, digital sharing, and home or professional printing; not for resale, redistribution, or commercial client work

## Creative and commercial direction

- Purchase trigger: The event needs to look cohesive, but the host feels short on time and uncertain about editing, exporting, and printing separate files.
- Primary sales hook: Personalize the invitation first, then follow one clear path to the shower day.
- Main buyer objection: “Will I be able to edit, download, and print this without Canva or design experience?”
- Original H4HappyLiving angle: A host-first sequence, calm file map, purposeful essentials, and tested print/digital guidance instead of an inflated template-count claim
- First-use success moment: In about 10 minutes, the customer personalizes the 5 x 7 invitation and mobile version and can choose the correct export for print or sharing.
- Perceived-value drivers: Cohesive cross-format design, obvious next steps, useful event-day coverage, answer keys, exact sizing, and dependable instructions
- Customer satisfaction safeguards: Quick-start index, editable-versus-fixed table, free-Canva compatibility check, desktop/mobile notes, exact sizes, export presets, answer keys, tested links, contents/exclusions, and troubleshooting
- Likely support questions: How to access Canva links; whether Canva Pro is required; what is editable; how to add a registry link or QR code; how to print at 5 x 7; which PDF option to choose; how to share the mobile invite; whether fonts/elements are free; and what is included
- Thumbnail promise: “Invitation-to-shower-day Canva kit,” supported by a clear invitation hero and three benefit cues: edit, print/share, host with confidence
- Listing-image story: Cohesive outcome -> who it fits -> exact contents -> 10-minute start -> print/share workflow -> host-first difference -> editable limits and exclusions

## Scope

### Included

- 5 x 7 invitation and 1080 x 1920 mobile invitation
- Books-for-baby, diaper-raffle, and registry/QR inserts
- Five essential signs: welcome, cards and gifts, favors, guest book/advice, and food/drink station
- Ten games: baby predictions, advice for parents, baby bingo, baby word scramble, baby name race, who knows the parents, emoji baby phrases, guess the baby food, wishes for baby, and over-or-under; final wording and mechanics must be original or based only on common game concepts
- Answer keys for word scramble, emoji phrases, and every other objectively scored game
- Food labels/place cards, favor tags, and thank-you card
- Host checklist/timeline, quick-start file map, editing disclosure, print/download guide, and troubleshooting
- Editable Canva template links plus a customer-facing access PDF

### Not included

- Physical printing, shipping, envelopes, paper, frames, props, or customization service
- Canva Pro subscription or paid Canva elements
- RSVP website, automated RSVP collection, email/SMS delivery, or registry hosting
- Character, celebrity, sports-team, franchise, or trademark themes
- Unlimited games, signs, or colorways; version 1 is one focused garden-brunch system
- Resale, redistribution, client-use, or commercial-use rights

## Design and copy

- Brand pillar: Create, supported by Organize
- Palette and typography: H4HappyLiving Evergreen `#285247`, Ink `#24332F`, Warm Cream `#FFF9F1`, Soft Sage `#B8D2C5`, and restrained Create Coral `#E77B68`; Nunito Sans for function, with Lora Italic only as a short editorial accent
- Accessibility requirements: Ink or Evergreen body text on Warm Cream/white; minimum practical 12 pt print body text and 16 px equivalent for instructional screens; sufficient contrast; no information communicated by color alone; no script font for essential details
- Voice: Warm, calm, practical, and specific; the host should feel capable, not pressured
- Claims or legal limitations: Digital product only; no physical item; Canva account required; free-Canva compatibility must be tested; color and print results vary; customer must test QR codes and event details; no commercial resale or redistribution

## Risks and validation

- Technical risks: Broken Canva template links, Pro-only elements, font substitution, cropped print exports, small phone text, QR-code failures, and inconsistent page sizes
- Customer-support risks: Confusion about digital delivery, Canva access, editable elements, mobile editing, printing choices, game answer keys, and exact inclusions
- Required test cases: Fresh free-Canva account; desktop and mobile access; long and short names; long venue/RSVP text; 5 x 7 print at 100%; two-up game printing; professional-printer PDF; mobile JPG readability; QR scan from screen and print; every answer key; every internal/customer link; download file names; and no paid-element watermark
- Evidence limitations: EverBee figures are estimates from a ranked or filtered export; live reference pages were not retrievable during setup; observed prices and review counts may change; customer testing is still required
