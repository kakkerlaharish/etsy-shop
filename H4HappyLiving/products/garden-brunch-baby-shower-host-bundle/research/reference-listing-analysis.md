# Reference Listing Analysis

Reviewed: 2026-08-02

## Method and boundaries

This analysis uses public listing information captured in the approved niche research. Direct live-page retrieval was unavailable during product setup, so prices, ratings, reviews, and contents are dated observations rather than current guarantees. No competitor files, artwork, layouts, copy, or templates were accessed or reproduced.

## Requirements derived from the market

1. **Completeness must feel navigable.** Organize the bundle into Start here, Invite, Prepare, and Shower day groups instead of a large flat asset list.
2. **Editing boundaries must be obvious.** State what text, colors, fonts, graphics, and QR content can be changed, and identify fixed artwork before purchase and in the quick-start guide.
3. **The first success must be fast and visible.** A new customer should personalize the 5 x 7 invitation and 1080 x 1920 mobile invitation and understand both export paths in about 10 minutes.
4. **The theme must support the experience.** Garden-brunch visuals should improve cohesion without reducing legibility, print reliability, or gender inclusivity.
5. **The kit must reduce host decisions.** Include a timeline, recommended order of use, concise sign/game choices, answer keys, and named export presets.
6. **Value must come from confidence, not inflated quantity.** Every component needs a clear use case and a place in the contents list; unsupported "everything" or "100+" claims are prohibited.

## Creative safeguards

- Do not trace, recreate, or closely imitate any competitor composition, illustration, copy, typography pairing, game arrangement, or listing-image sequence.
- Use licensed Canva Free elements or original assets only; record any element whose license or free-account availability must be checked.
- Avoid characters, franchises, trademarks, celebrity likenesses, and distinctive branded event themes.
- Treat reference listings as evidence of buyer needs and functional patterns only.
