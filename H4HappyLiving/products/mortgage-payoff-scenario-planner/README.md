## Customer problem

US homeowners want to understand whether extra principal payments are worthwhile without building or auditing mortgage formulas themselves.

## Product promise

Enter a fixed-rate mortgage once, compare two extra-payment strategies against the normal schedule, and see the projected payoff date, months saved, and estimated interest difference.

## Key decisions

- US fixed-rate, monthly-payment mortgage only in version 1.
- Separate baseline, planned strategy, and alternative strategy.
- No tax, insurance, escrow, refinancing, balloon, or variable-rate calculations.
- Visible model checks and plain-language assumptions reduce calculation and support risk.
