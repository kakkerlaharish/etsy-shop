## Original product direction

The H4HappyLiving product will use an original layout, brand system, formula implementation, naming, and instructions. It will not reproduce competitor formulas, files, text, imagery, or distinctive presentation.

The prototype should win on:

1. decision clarity — baseline versus two named strategies;
2. calculation trust — model checks and explicit exclusions;
3. onboarding — obvious input cells and a short guided flow;
4. motivation — debt-free date, months saved, interest difference, and a balance chart.
