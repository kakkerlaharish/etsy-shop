# Baby Shower Invitations Niche Analysis

Research date: 2026-08-01
Market: Etsy US
Source period: EverBee last 1 month export plus public Etsy evidence

## 1. Decision

**Recommendation: GO**  
**Opportunity score: 78/100**  
**Confidence: Medium**

Start digital-first with a narrow, event-driven offer: an original gender-neutral storybook / “A New Chapter Begins” editable baby-shower invitation suite. Demand is visible in the supplied EverBee export and recent entrants are selling, but competition is high and the estimates come from a selected EverBee result set rather than a complete Etsy census. This is a focused launch test, not a recommendation to enter generic printables.

## 3. Competitor Patterns

- Editable Canva invitations dominate, with 5x7 print and mobile-sharing use cases communicated immediately.
- Matching book-request and diaper-raffle cards create a clear suite and support pricing around the $9.50 direct-listing median.
- Common SEO language includes “baby shower invite,” “editable template,” “digital invitation,” “Canva template,” and “instant download.”
- Public listings emphasize free Canva access, demos, purchase-edit-download instructions, and print/send options.
- The recurring weakness is sameness: stacks of watercolor books, bows, florals, and heavy discounting. Copyrighted-character themes are also a material avoidable risk.

## 5. Recommended Product

**Product:** A New Chapter Begins — editable storybook baby-shower invitation mini-suite  
**Customer:** A host planning a warm, gender-neutral book-themed shower who needs a coordinated invitation quickly  
**Promise:** Edit, download, and send a polished invitation in about 10 minutes  
**Purchase trigger:** The shower date and theme are chosen and invitations need to go out now  
**Primary sales hook:** One cohesive storybook suite for print and phone sharing  
**Original H4HappyLiving angle:** A modern “family library” identity using original bookplate-inspired typography, chapter dividers, and botanical linework—not copied watercolor stacks or protected characters  
**First-use success moment:** The buyer opens the free Canva link, replaces the sample details, and exports the mobile invite without needing support  
**Customer satisfaction safeguards:** Demo link; exact editable/non-editable disclosure; free-Canva compatibility; print and mobile sizes; export instructions; bleed guidance; fictional sample details; clear digital-only notice  
**Launch price:** $8.95  
**Regular test price:** $11.95

Minimum viable product:

- 5x7 editable invitation
- 1080x1920 mobile invitation
- 5x3.5 book-request card
- 5x3.5 optional diaper-raffle/details card

## 7. Risks and Next Action

Main risks:

1. High competition, aggressive discounting, and easy-to-copy visual conventions can suppress visibility and price.
2. EverBee data is estimated and the export is selection-biased; the apparent 100% non-zero sales rate must not be treated as a marketplace conversion rate.
3. Copyrighted characters are common: 168 direct listings were flagged for potential IP risk. H4HappyLiving should use only original or properly licensed assets.

**Next action:** Approve or reject this concept. If approved, analyze 2-3 reference listings for functional strengths and customer gaps, then create original product requirements before generating any product files. Treat POD as a separate validation gate after the digital suite produces real clicks, favorites, and sales.
