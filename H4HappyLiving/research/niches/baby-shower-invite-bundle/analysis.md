## 1. Decision

**Recommendation: TEST**  
**Opportunity score: 72/100**  
**Confidence: Medium**

Build one focused, non-IP **Garden Brunch Baby Shower Host Bundle** rather than a generic mega-bundle. Demand is strong and spread across many sellers, but competition, discounting, and buyer expectations are high. H4HappyLiving can compete through a calm host-first workflow, clear setup, and dependable print/digital guidance—not by claiming the largest template count.

## 3. Competitor Patterns

- Invitation suites had a $15.14 median price; complete event bundles had a $30.02 median and higher estimated revenue per listing.
- Canva editing, printable PDFs, mobile invitations, insert cards, games, and signs are expected features. Discounts of 25%–70% are common.
- Theme-led titles dominate. The leading direct-segment tags included `baby shower bundle`, `baby shower invite`, `baby shower games`, `girl baby shower`, `baby shower decor`, and `editable template`.
- Buyers repeatedly praised completeness, easy Canva editing, cohesive appearance, and quick access. The sampled reviews revealed no recurring material complaint.
- Sellers devote substantial copy to access, editable elements, Canva use, and printing. These are predictable confusion and support risks. Interactive website invitations are an adjacent future threat.
- The top ten listings represented only 8.8% of estimated direct sales, indicating demand is not concentrated in a few winners. About 9.4% of direct listings contained apparent protected-character or brand references; H4HappyLiving should avoid that risk.

## 5. Recommended Product

**Product:** Garden Brunch Baby Shower Host Bundle for Canva  
**Customer:** A host planning a polished, modern shower who wants one coordinated system without design or file-management stress  
**Promise:** Coordinate the invitation and essential shower materials quickly from one clear Canva kit  
**Purchase trigger:** The host wants the event to look cohesive but feels overwhelmed by designing and organizing separate pieces  
**Primary sales hook:** One calm Canva kit that takes the host from invitation to shower day  
**Original H4HappyLiving angle:** A guided host workflow, calm file map, purposeful asset set, and tested print/digital instructions instead of a quantity-first “100+ templates” claim  
**First-use success moment:** Within about ten minutes, the customer can personalize the invitation and mobile version and understand exactly how to print or share them  
**Customer satisfaction safeguards:** Quick-start page, file index, editable-versus-fixed disclosure, free-Canva compatibility note, desktop/mobile guidance, print sizes, export instructions, game answer keys, tested links, and explicit contents/exclusions  
**Launch price:** $14.99  
**Regular test price:** $24.00

Minimum viable product:

- Editable 5×7 invitation and 1080×1920 mobile invitation
- Books-for-baby, diaper-raffle, and registry/QR insert cards
- Coordinated welcome sign and essential event signs
- Eight to ten high-use games with answer keys
- Food labels/place cards, favor tags, and thank-you card
- Host checklist/timeline, quick-start file map, and print/download guide

## 7. Risks and Next Action

Main risks:

1. Saturated themes, deep discounting, and large bundles make a generic offer difficult to communicate or defend.
2. Canva access, editing limits, printing, exports, and broken links can create poor reviews unless tested and explained precisely.
3. EverBee metrics are estimates and the export's coverage appears filtered; a narrow launch test is safer than a broad product line.

**Next action:** Approve this focused `TEST`; then analyze two to three reference listings and convert their functional strengths and customer gaps into an original product brief before design begins.
