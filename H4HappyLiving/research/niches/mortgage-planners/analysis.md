# Mortgage Planners Niche Analysis

Research date: 2026-07-30  
Market: Etsy US  
EverBee source: one-month export dated 2026-07-31

## 1. Decision

**Recommendation: TEST**  
**Opportunity score: 62/100**  
**Confidence: Medium**

Do not launch a broad “mortgage planner.” Test a focused **US mortgage payoff calculator spreadsheet**. Demand exists, but it is concentrated in a small number of listings. The clearest opportunity is a reliable, beginner-friendly tool that compares extra-payment scenarios and explains the results visually.

## 3. Competitor Patterns

- The main format is an Excel or Google Sheets calculator with amortization, payoff tracking, and extra-payment modeling.
- Direct-fit products cluster around $6.99-$9.99, although aggressive discounting creates a low-price tier.
- Successful positioning emphasizes years saved, estimated interest saved, payoff date, and visible progress.
- Buyers praise prebuilt formulas, time savings, dashboards, simple layouts, and tutorial videos.
- Reported friction includes unclear setup, locale-related calculation problems, and dependence on seller support.
- Generic debt-payoff tools are more established than mortgage-only products but create a less focused customer promise.

## 5. Recommended Product

**Product:** Mortgage Payoff Scenario Planner for Excel and Google Sheets  
**Customer:** US homeowners with a fixed-rate mortgage who are considering additional principal payments  
**Promise:** Compare extra-payment options and clearly see the projected effect on payoff time and interest.

Minimum viable product:

- Quick-start instructions and an example-filled version
- Mortgage assumptions and payment inputs
- Standard amortization schedule
- Recurring and one-time extra-payment inputs
- Side-by-side payoff scenarios
- Projected payoff date and estimated interest difference
- Progress dashboard
- Payment tracker
- Clear input validation and reset instructions
- Informational-use and calculation-assumption disclaimer

**Launch price:** $6.99  
**Regular test price:** $8.99

Do not add refinancing, variable-rate, tax, escrow, or international mortgage logic to the first version. Those features increase accuracy risk and customer-support requirements.

## 7. Risks and Next Action

Main risks:

1. Financial formulas require rigorous test cases and clearly stated assumptions.
2. Spreadsheet locale and compatibility problems can create a high support burden.
3. EverBee sales and revenue figures are estimates, and the export contains substantial off-niche noise.

**Next action:** Build and independently validate a small calculation prototype before investing in branded design. Test standard amortization, one-time extra payments, recurring extra payments, zero-interest edge cases, and payoff truncation. Proceed to product development only if the prototype is accurate in both Excel and Google Sheets.
